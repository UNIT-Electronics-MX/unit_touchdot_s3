## **6 Mechanical Information**

![](hardware/resources/unit_dimension_V_0_1_2_ue0072_Touch-Dot-S3.png){width=5.8in}

All dimensions are in millimeters unless otherwise noted. Use the released
dimension drawing as the controlling reference for enclosure, textile, and
mounting design. Allow clearance for the USB-C plug, battery connector,
microSD card insertion, power switch, and any components soldered to the
sewable pads or expansion headers.
